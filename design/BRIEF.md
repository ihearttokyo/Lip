# Lip visual direction

Selected reference: `lip-reference.png`, generated with the built-in GPT image
tool on 2026-10-07, then inspected before UI implementation.

The design follows Wispr Flow's pale background, editorial typography and
floating dictation controls, with original Lip artwork and wording. Use butter
`#FFFEEB`, lavender `#E6D5FB`, ink `#191A16`, violet `#6F43B5` and white.
Large serif headlines anchor the home screen and landing page; controls use
system sans-serif. The waveform pill is the central product cue. Avoid copied
logos, testimonials, performance claims and proprietary assets.

The image is a design reference, not a screenshot or a capability certificate.
The implementation must label OAuth eligibility and hardware-dependent speech
support, and must not promise cloud audio through ChatGPT-plan authorization.
