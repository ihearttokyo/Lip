Privacy, in plain words.

Lip v0.2.0 prerelease · Updated October 8, 2026

Lip recognizes speech on your Android device. Optional ChatGPT cleanup sends the resulting text to OpenAI. Your local history and sign-in credentials are encrypted on your phone.

This describes the prerelease implementation, not a completed independent security audit. Real-device continuous speech, editor compatibility, and end-to-end ChatGPT authorization are not yet live-validated. Read the source before enabling accessibility access.

Microphone and speech

Lip requests Android microphone permission. Recognition starts only after you tap a dictation control and has a visible listening state. Lip uses Android's explicit on-device SpeechRecognizer and does not upload audio or save recording files. It is never always-listening. An explicitly started session can continue across apps until Finish, Cancel or a protective interruption. Continuous mode requires a provider supporting supplied-audio segmented recognition.

Your phone needs a compatible on-device recognition service and an available language model. If that model is missing, Lip reports it instead of silently choosing a cloud recognizer. Your device supplies the recognition service. Review your device vendor's settings and policies. See Android's SpeechRecognizer documentation.

Accessibility and the current editor

The accessibility service finds a focused editable field, shows the floating dictation bubble, and inserts text through Android's accessibility input connection. Field text, selection, and editor information are checked locally to verify that the target has not changed. Surrounding editor content is never included in an OpenAI request.

Lip refuses password and protected fields and blocks stale insertion after a focus, cursor, or content change. After switching apps, only an explicit Insert here action chooses a fresh eligible target. An unconfirmed dispatched commit is not retried. Capture stops on locking, protected-field focus, interruption or loss of the bound accessibility service. It does not click through other apps, send messages, or automate navigation. You can enable review-before-insert to check each result inside the floating bubble, or disable Lip entirely in Android's accessibility settings.

Optional ChatGPT cleanup

When you enable ChatGPT cleanup, Lip sends the transcript and the cleanup instructions needed for your chosen style to OpenAI's public Responses API. Dictionary terms may be included to help preserve names and terminology. Audio, surrounding editor text, and your transcript-history collection are not included. When separately enabled, live preview sends stable segments before you finish. New sign-in discloses this behavior; upgrading does not enable it on an existing installation. Disable Live ChatGPT preview in Settings to send only the final transcript. Cancel cannot recall requests already sent. Preview requests consume your plan allowance.

Lip uses store:false and stream:true, as required by this preview. These request settings do not constitute a promise of zero provider retention or no provider processing. OpenAI's policies and your account or workspace terms govern processing by OpenAI. Cleanup access, available models, and usage limits depend on your account. If cleanup fails or a stream is incomplete, Lip keeps the raw transcript available.

This integration does not grant audio transcription access and does not read your ChatGPT conversation history. Consult OpenAI's inference documentation, preview limitations, and OpenAI's privacy policy.

ChatGPT sign-in

Continue with ChatGPT opens the system browser for official OAuth authorization. Lip registers a client for your installation and uses PKCE, a local loopback callback, state checks, and identity-token validation. You enter your password in OpenAI's browser flow, never in Lip.

Lip retains the issued client registration, host identifier, validated account information, and OAuth credentials needed to use and refresh the connection. Credentials are encrypted in app-private storage using Android Keystore-backed AES-GCM and excluded from Android backup. Sign out in Lip to clear local OAuth tokens and attempt provider-side token revocation. Lip retains account and client-registration metadata for later reconnection. If remote revocation fails, revoke authorization separately in your account controls. Official registration and sign-in documentation.

History, dictionary, and deletion

History is enabled by default. It stores raw and cleaned transcripts in encrypted app-private storage on this device. History lets you search, copy, delete individual entries, or clear all entries. Disable history in Settings to stop saving new entries; that does not automatically erase existing history. History uses independent encrypted records rather than one capped collection file. Individual records have a safety size limit and available device storage limits total capacity. Verified migration preserves v0.1 entries, but the old APK cannot read the new format; do not uninstall or clear app data to downgrade. A failed save is reported and the current text stays available.

Your dictionary and preferences stay local. Dictionary terms may accompany a cleanup request when ChatGPT cleanup is enabled. There is no Lip account, cloud history service, or cross-device sync. Uninstalling removes the app's local storage; this release does not provide a history export or recovery service.

Copying text places it on Android's clipboard, outside Lip's encrypted storage. Pasting it into another app subjects that text to the destination app's behavior and policies. Deleting local history does not retract text already copied, inserted, or sent to OpenAI.

Logs, analytics, and this website

Lip includes no analytics or advertising SDK. It does not intentionally log transcripts, audio, or credentials. Android and the device's recognition service may generate their own diagnostics.

This website is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not request microphone permission, connect to ChatGPT, set application cookies, or save your demo choices. The before-and-after demo uses prepared text and accesses the clipboard only after you press Copy example. It uses local assets and system fonts, with no third-party font or analytics requests. GitHub processes hosting traffic under GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions and changes

Lip is maintained by Jared Bland. Use the project's issue tracker for questions or bugs, but do not post private transcripts, sign-in codes, tokens, or other secrets. Privacy-relevant changes should update this page and the release notes.
