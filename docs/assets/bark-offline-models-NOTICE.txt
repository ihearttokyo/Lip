The Bark family derives from NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, copyright NVIDIA,
and Moondream parakeet-ultra, copyright Moondream, under CC BY 4.0:
https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-ultra
Conway Research post-trained/compressed the models; Scholz converted/quantized
them to GGUF. Lip packages these existing derivatives unchanged, integrates a
modified Starling/GGML runtime, and does not claim the publisher's BF16 benchmark
results apply to this quantized CPU preview. Speech recognition is English-only.

The separate Starling, GGML, neural keyboard/data and application licenses are
retained alongside these notices. No Google/Gboard or WeChat proprietary artwork
or models are included as part of this speech-model bundle.
