Set up the floating bubble
  1. 1

    Tap, then talk.

    Focus a supported text field and tap the Lip bubble. Recording starts only after your action, with a visible listening state.

  2. 2

    Choose how it reads.

    Watch your local transcript as you speak. Opt into live ChatGPT cleanup for stable segments, then choose Polished, Light, or Verbatim and add names to your dictionary.

  3. 3

    Put your words in place.

    Insert into the original editor, or enable review-before-insert to check each result in the bubble first. If the field or cursor changes, Lip holds the text. Review it, then explicitly choose Insert here in your intended field.

A small app. A few deliberate permissions.

Make room for your voice.

Android 13 or newer.
Keep your current keyboard.

  1. Install the prerelease APK.

    Download Lip v0.2.0 from this repository. Allow installation from your browser or file manager only if you trust the file; turn that permission off afterward.

  2. Enable the microphone and bubble.

    Open Lip, read the separate permission disclosures, then grant microphone access and enable Lip in Android's accessibility settings. Accessibility lets Lip find the editor and insert text.

  3. Check your language model.

    Choose English, Japanese, or Mandarin. Local recognition requires a compatible on-device service and an available model for that language. Lip does not send audio to a cloud fallback.

  4. Try local dictation first.

    Open a supported editor, tap the bubble, speak, and check the result. Turn on review-before-insert if you want to approve each phrase. Connect ChatGPT only if you want transcript cleanup; account eligibility is required.

Know what you're enabling.

Accessibility is a powerful Android permission. Enable it only after reviewing the app and its source. Lip's intended use is user-triggered dictation, not autonomous navigation or message sending.

Android may show an extra restricted-settings confirmation for a sideloaded accessibility app. Follow the system's user-controlled prompts only if you trust Lip. Do not disable Play Protect.

Before you download.

Do I have to switch away from Gboard?

No. Lip uses Android 13's accessibility input connection while your keyboard stays selected. Some custom or protected editors may not accept insertion. Lip should hold the transcript rather than bypass their restrictions. Android's input-method documentation explains the underlying API.

Does it work offline?

Continuous recognition uses local PCM and Android's explicit on-device segmented recognizer. A compatible provider must honor supplied-audio continuous mode; Lip reports unsupported or early-ended sessions instead of silently restarting with gaps. It works only when the device has a compatible recognition service and the selected language model is available. ChatGPT cleanup needs a network connection. If cleanup fails, keep the local transcript. Android's recognizer documentation describes these device-dependent requirements.

Can I use my ChatGPT subscription?

Optional cleanup uses official Sign in with ChatGPT and the public Responses API. Choose Continue with ChatGPT in Lip. Your system browser handles sign-in and authorization, and Lip registers its own client for that installation. You need an eligible account. Preview access, models, and usage limits may change; a plan is not a promise of unlimited cleanup. This path does not provide audio transcription. See OpenAI's preview limitations and the sign-in instructions.

Where do my recordings and transcripts go?

Lip does not upload or save audio recordings. History stores raw and cleaned text encrypted in app-private storage on your device; you can disable history or delete it. When you choose ChatGPT cleanup, your transcript is sent to OpenAI. Copying text puts it on Android's clipboard, outside Lip's encrypted storage. See the privacy policy.

Will Lip send messages for me?

No. Lip inserts dictated text into an editor; sending remains your action. Review-before-insert is an optional setting. It is not an autonomous assistant and does not navigate other apps. Password and protected fields are excluded, and a focus or cursor change blocks stale insertion.
