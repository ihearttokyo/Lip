Product captures

The keyboard, emoji and GIF images are unmodified native captures from Lip's Android development build on an emulator. Editors contain synthetic test text. The page crops details with CSS; the full originals open from each image. These are not physical-phone, macOS or Gboard screenshots.

Screenshot sources and SHA-256 identities. The decorative Fn key and microphone cue are interface illustrations, not Mac screenshots or a live recording interface.
